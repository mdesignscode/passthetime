## Direction contract

THESIS: Random Stuff is a street vending machine; every activity and item sits in a coded slot. It refuses the headline over a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cobalt baked-enamel body, lit cool-white glass compartments, black LED displays in red-orange dot lettering, shelf rails carrying one real fact, plus a code on slots the keypad can reach, goods in saturated packets, thermal paper receipts for reading. Yellow is reserved for the selected slot and focus. Night mode is the same machine in the dark, lit by its own compartments.

STORY: A reviewer grasps the machine at once, punches a code or clicks a slot, sees it dispense, then finds each activity behaves as one machine.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim header with logo, coded links, keypad toggle, lights rocker. Full-width LED marquee with the h1 at large scale. Below, a glass wall of five unequal lit compartments with real goods fills about three quarters; a control column with display, working keypad and retrieval flap holds the rest. The primary action is any slot.
